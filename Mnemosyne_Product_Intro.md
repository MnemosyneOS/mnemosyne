## 🌟 In One Sentence

Before calling any LLM, Mnemosyne pre-filters massive memory stores down to the Top-10 most relevant items at **zero compute cost** — saving 80%+ tokens with <10ms latency. But it's far more than token savings: it's the world's only single-file, zero-dependency, fully-local AI memory engine, with native 7-language support, 8+ Agent frameworks, knowledge-graph reasoning, and automatic reflection. Copy one .py file, and your AI gains persistent long-term memory.
