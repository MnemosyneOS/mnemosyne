# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We review code, not people. Criticism of an implementation is welcome;
criticism of a contributor is not.

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Assuming competence and asking for the reasoning behind a decision
- Accepting that "this does not fit the project's constraints" is a valid
  outcome, and saying so plainly
- Writing issues that a stranger could act on without asking follow-ups

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Insults, personal attacks, or sustained hostility
- Publishing another contributor's private information
- Sustained off-topic disruption of issues, discussions or pull requests

## Scope

This applies in the project's repositories, issues, pull requests and any
other project space.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainers through
[ai-memory.net/#/contact](https://ai-memory.net/#/contact). All reports will
be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed
necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers who do not follow
or enforce the code of conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
